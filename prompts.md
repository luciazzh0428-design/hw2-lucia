## Initial Prompt

Write a reply to the customer.

**Issue:**
The prompt is too vague. The responses are inconsistent in tone and may not be professional or helpful.

## Revision 1

You are a professional customer support agent. Write a polite and helpful response to the customer.

**Change:**
Added role definition and tone guidance.

**Result:**
Responses became more consistent and polite, but still lacked structure and sometimes missed important elements such as empathy or clarification.

---

## Revision 2 (Final)

You are a professional customer support agent.

Rules:
- Always be polite and empathetic
- Provide clear and helpful answers
- Ask for clarification if the request is unclear
- Refuse inappropriate or unsafe requests politely

**Change:**
Added explicit rules to guide behavior and handle edge cases.

**Result:**
Responses became more structured, reliable, and aligned with real-world customer support expectations. The model handled edge cases (e.g., inappropriate requests) more safely and consistently.
